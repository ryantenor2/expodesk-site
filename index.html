FAQ

Questions people ask at the stand

Do my leads' details stay private?

Yes. Every workspace is isolated — your leads are visible only to you and teammates you explicitly connect with. Photos and files live behind authentication, GPS metadata is stripped from every image, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

Does it really work offline?

Fully, for the part that matters: capturing. Leads — photos and voice notes included — queue on your phone and sync automatically when signal returns. AI reading and research run right after.

Do I need to install an app?

No store, no IT department: Expodesk runs in the browser and installs to your home screen in two taps, on iPhone and Android alike. Native apps are on the roadmap.

What languages do the cards need to be in?

Any. The reader handles Latin, Arabic, Cyrillic and mixed-script cards — it was field-tested on a Saudi trade show with cards in three alphabets.

How do the AI credits work?

Simple metering: one credit is one euro cent. A card read costs about 5 credits, a deep web research about 25. Plans include an allowance; top-ups are transparent, no subscriptions hidden inside subscriptions.

How do I install it on my phone?

Open app.expodesk.app, then: iPhone — Share → View More → Add to Home Screen; Android — menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. Ten seconds, no app store. Watch the 10-second video.
